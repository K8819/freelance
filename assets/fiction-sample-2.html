Best of All Possible Burritos

Fiction • By Adam Krieger • 2026

​

In nineteenth-century America…there was a social convention called the "barbecue law," which meant that all the men who gathered for a barbecue were expected to drink until they were soused. To refuse a drink entailed a serious insult to the host and the rest of the party.

— Willpower: Rediscovering the Greatest Human Strength

​

The dentist sat quietly waiting for the drugs to kick in before he began to irrigate the gums. His patient sat, waiting in the highchair as a menagerie of high pitched squeals and fumes of gas began to fill the air...

​

I woke up barefoot in a stupor on the middle of the railroad tracks. My olive oil surplus jacket laid beneath me with my shoes pinch hitting as a pillow. The sky was burning my face and I couldn't wait to shed my skin.

​

I wasn't there for too long, the trains run early every morning. I'd be dead by now if this wasn't hell already.

​

"That could have ended badly. Must be my lucky day!"

​

If there was ever a time to celebrate it was then. Abbey's Cafe it was. I checked my pockets. Phone, wallet, $20, five-pound piece of steel rebar, peppermint Binaca spray, and a Zippo lighter inside an empty package of baloney.

​

"A bunch of useless shit. I think that's everything."

​

Fuck, it was Christmas! Merry goddamn new year I guess. I could tell I'd been drinking expensive whiskey because my breath smelled like fermented cheese and honey-bacon.

​

It was a dog day for sure. My throat was so dry I couldn't swallow so I used the Binaca. That shit is 43% alcohol it just made things worse. I had a 10-mile walk ahead of me. I flung my jacket around my shoulders like a cape and flew away.

​

Albeit not quite real flying, I was too weak to do anything but hover slightly above sea level, with my head bobbing up and down like counterweight.

The Perfect Bite

​

My perfect burrito is a chorizo and egg. I couldn't stop thinking about opening my present and sinking my gums into those spicy, juicy clouds of fluff wrapped up in a burnt up buttery blanket. Softly beaten childhood memories groaned deep inside my belly. My stomach growled like a baby in a bottle.

​

"Man, I should've worn shorts."

​

"Only bashful people don't wear shorts," is what my pet bear would say whenever we met in the woods. "You need meat on your booones," which was true enough to make me angry. Maybe I should bash her face in and eat her. I could live for years off that sow. I could get wasted off her blood. She was a nurse who was addicted to the poison and the cure. The hospital scene and the drugs she dolled out. Even after she was fired for stealing pills and finished rehab, her insurance covered an implanted Dilauded pump and a delectable slew of stomach ulcerated mind-altering drugs.

​

"Due for a heart attack any minute," I muttered to myself. I couldn't wait for the coca-cola and ham sandwiches to strangle her arteries.

​

"What the fuck am I thinking? This kind of shit doesn't pop into my head unless I'm seriously dehydrated or hungry.

​

She was terrible and kind to everybody. I wasn't sure if she really bothered me or I had contracted diabetes. One thing was for sure, Abbey's Cafe was open on holidays and they had the best breakfast in town. There were still a lot of elderly folks living in old folks' homes that eat there every Christmas. It was a good place to be on a day like today.

​

The polyester sun was golden brown. It was always dry and hot in that shitty suburban sprawl located 180 miles south of LA, in the asshole of Riverside county. Don't get me wrong, it was a great place to live, plenty of natural beauty. But not if you had to walk everywhere.

​

I was near an Indian reservation. The place was a cesspool of rotten apples, if you farted it would probably improve the air quality. They'd find more bodies in the riverbeds underneath the freeways every few weeks and Orange County would hand out week-long hotel vouchers and ship them over the county line by the dozens.

​

After a few years of this, the homeless population grew by 1000% and halfway houses popped up everywhere. The Sheriffs departments were making a killing of subsidies and offender programs. But a funny thing happened. Underneath all the topsoil and sifted sand, wells of freshwater began to spring everywhere.

Obtrusive Interstitial

​

I walked down the street, lined with Cypress trees on the devil strips past the shabby houses constructed in the 1980s. A former planned community held together by rope, wood, hard tack and desert dirt. I eased on down the road...my liver was hard (I couldn't mow the yard), and my back and legs ached from inhibition.

​

"They need to make a fence around that railroad track before some dildo kills himself by accident."

​

I stopped to catch a glimpse of myself in a car window, I looked like hell. I looked like the living dead all right. I did my best impression of Buffalo Bill, "I'd still fuck me."

​

I tried to spit at my reflection but I had no ammo. As I teethed the dry skin off my lips, a dog started barking at me from behind a four-foot fence...some kinda bull terrier mut creature with 6 saggy breasts and a golden chain around her neck.

​

"It's okay wittle tail chaser," I spoke in a dulcet tone. "Yeah whadda mean mutt you are." I think I patronized her because she seemed offended. She began to compulsively spin around and self-mutilate in between shrieks of panic. OCD. She reminded me of an article I read called, Why Your Dog Keeps Humping Things.

​

"Easy momma. All right? I'm scared you are scary please shut up."

​

I've been attacked by my share of house pets, I wasn't in immediate danger or this bitch would be on the fence or over it because she wasn't chained up. I went back to business and took another glance in the mirror to fix myself up a little before I went on my merry way...

​

Then I heard a voice like the crack of a whip, the dogs pet, "You tryin to break into my car mothafucka!"

​

Now I was in danger. I turned around and saw a doublewide 6'6" black dude storm out of the house. I thought about the big dent in the hood of his car. He probably body slammed the last drifter on it. I probably enjoyed it too.

​

I broke out in a sweat. I thought about the rebar in my pocket; if I hit him with it and messed up it would just make him angrier. "No, no, no, no, no," I stammered. "I thought it was for sale." Why I would say that is beyond me, of all things just tell the truth. I started to run and my side started to ache.

​

"That's allright mothafucka, I'll be right after you in my car!" said the refrigerator of a man protecting his dog. What a beautiful relationship they must have, dysfunctional, but beautiful.

Distracted From Distraction by Distraction

​

Shit! Shit! Shit! I ran a couple of blocks and ducked into an alley behind the laundromat. I sunk into the wall by the dumpster. My heartbeat was audible as it turned my shirt into a quivering drumhead.

​

I noticed I was near the section 8 apartments. Closer to my prize, "Chorizo and eggs, I want you in my mouth."

​

I used some more Binaca to wet my whistle, which was probably a mistake. It burned as I tried to swallow. My teeth scraped at the cracked dead skin on my lips out of instinct. I slumped down against the wall beside the dumpster to collect myself and then I heard it.

​

"Eyyy man, ey man, what the fuck do you think you are doing!?!"

​

Did he find me already? No, it wasn't the same voice. It was much more depraved and disheveled. It was a haggard bum. He was yelling at nothing. "You think you're smarter than me birdbrain! You think your smarter than me? Get away from me you evil motherfucker!"

​

His pants were falling down and just like that, he jumped right out of them and began to hover 6 feet above the ground.

​

"Fuckin' crackhead," I said. I thought I was bad but he's completely lost his marbles. He dropped the chain scurried off around the corner of the building. Anyways, I thought I'd better go grab the chain just in case the green mile comes back to kill me.

​

I bent down to pick it up and the second I turned around, it felt like someone landed a fishhook in my right nostril, it just barely missed my eye. Just as I palmed my face with both hands something sharp hit me in the crotch. I started swinging the chain and running back toward the dumpster. Blood from my mons pubis soaked through my jeans. My outfit changed into that of a hoe boy panning for gold. My beard curled up as my naked feet searched the ground.

​

A small gang of blackbirds were doing their darnedest to peck me to death, at least four of them. They always work in fours. I dropped the chain and used my jacket to swat them down, it was sorta working. They backed off me but were now dive bombing me from all sides.

​

I ran back to the dumpster but quickly doubled back to grab the chain as they continued to punish me. I got back to the dumpster, jumped inside, and shut the lid. Darkness.

​

I could hear their terrible little claws scratching the plastic lid. There were six of them now speaking in song via high pitched gurgling, "We wait, haha! We fuck him up, haha! Haw! Haw! Haw!"

Oriental Dungeon

​

This is how I'm going to die? Suffocate to death in a trash chamber? The stench of burnt carbon and used dryer sheets mixed with my own scent was going to slowly gas me out. The place was less than half full but I was comfortable squatting on the trash bags full of food and unwanted clothing.

​

Ropes of light were shining in from outside. I was kind of beautiful. I felt incredibly special at that moment. A crack in the sky and a hand reaching down to me... austere beauty, my positive correlation. I was doomed, thirsty for endless pleasure. An aesthetic without faith or morals. "This isn't so bad, why didn't I sleep in here? With all the beautiful trash?"

​

I ripped open a black trash bag and opened a styrofoam container. "Chili cheese fries? Don't mind if I do." I ate a couple but I was queasy, didn't want to push my luck. The rest were my ticket outta there because birds love french fries.

​

Just then, one of the beams of light illuminated something shiny. It was a discarded wind chime with an inscription on it...

​

In his activities, he loves timeliness It is because he does not compete that he is without reproach — Lao-Tzu Chinese Philosopher

​

As true as it was. At the moment I thought, unlike most of the other trash, it belonged in the dumpster. What a load of shit, Lao Tzu, Santa Claus, Thomas Edison. Orientalism for clueless hippies. Even D.T. Suzuki admitted he was an imperialist and favored campaigns for racial cleansing. The politics were beyond me but at least he advertised Buddhism as a technique for meditation rather than a civil religion.

​

In my eyes, truth and beauty had been minced so many times that I couldn't tell the truth from a sore on my lip. Isn't war a lie? Is culling natural? Samsara, Nirvana? I'd gone soft in the head. I threw it against the metal wall and the birds started squawking again. There were more of them...a lot more. Were they holding a caucus?

​

What the hell did I do yesterday? What set this in motion? Why were they waiting for me to come out? Crows never forget a face. There was a popular study on the common American crow (colloquially known as Corvus brachyrhynchos), called Lasting recognition of threatening people by wild American crows. It basically demonstrated that they never forget a face, but could be easily fooled with a rubber mask from a far enough distance.

​

No matter how many people were wearing the masks the crows would classify them as dangerous. Was someone walking around with my face? A dead ringer. Maybe it was my twin brother. He's always killing small animals for some expedition or another. Last week he came over and hit a frog with a rock from thirty-paces away, for no apparent reason. Impressive but a shitty thing to do in this book. Wild things are stoic. The frog would pity me.

​

As I sat still, I began to puke. Bits and pieces of the night came back to me. "Oh god no!" I couldn't even look at my phone, who knows what the hell I said or did. What if I got stoned and ate a bird or something? I swore off meat until lent was over, but I had no idea what year it was, let alone which season.

​

I switched my phone on. "Heyyyy, half charged, nice." Things weren't so bad after all; I could fondle this thing for another three hours. I vigorously swiped up and down until I came to a conclusion and my head was clear.

Eyes Kant See It

​

"I'm going to miss this dumpster. Why didn't I sleep here last night? The Best of All Possible dumpsters." I pretended I was in the mountains where I used to stay. I'd shoegaze during the day and stargaze naked underneath a free bed of leaves at night. Then wake up soaking wet in the middle of the night. The sweet smell of maple leaves and cherry alder wood soaking into my pores. Breidablik... home of my lord Balder. Nordic Eden.

​

I left because Eden was boring. So I took off to wander the desert with my Eve. We were so bored we had children. Then I got so bored I began searching for the best burritos in the desert. Chorizo and eggs are certainly not boring. I could eat them for the rest of my life.

​

The birds had seemed to calm down, I figured now was my chance. I took out my lighter and the Binaca. Then stood up slowly, raising the hood with the crown of my skull. Like flies buzzing around a piece of shit, they hovered near the open lid.

​

I sparked my zippo and began to pop up and down like a jack in the box, pumping the Binaca at the lit flame until it exploded like a mini flamethrower. I did this over and over until I was out of fuel, then quickly abandoned that plan. All it did was burn the hair on my knuckles, which I lapped up like cotton candy.

​

I popped up again and slammed the dumpster open and threw the chili fries as far away as I could. The whole dozen of them fought over the fries. I didn't stick the landing; landing hard on my right side and screamed out, "That fucking chain!" The rebar rolled under the dumpster to the magnets on the wall.

​

I looked up and realized I was fucked. There were 40 or 50 of them lined up on both sides of the alley rooftops, ready to pounce. "Cawk, Cawk, Cawk!" They were nagging me from all sides with a New Jersey nanny housewife accent. I was in a circle of shame.

​

I wrapped the chain around my neck and made my move. I swung my jacket around wildly as they swarmed and ran towards Abbey's...

​

Everything moved in slow motion. They were stabbing the fuck out of me from everywhere but I was so high on adrenaline that I enjoyed it. At that moment I felt incredibly rejected somehow. I loved black birds, especially audible crows. I nursed one to health when I was younger and it followed me to school every morning until I moved away.

​

I dropped my jacket and swung the chain around screaming; I had shrunk into a 14-year old boy renouncing his puberty.

​

"You think you're smart birdbrains! You think you are smarter than me? Fuck you! Get away from me you stupid tits!"

​

I used my jacket like a net to catch one and ripped its wing off. The blood and bone and hair and meat burst down my throat, nourishing my heart with surprises and salty gushy tar. A moral victory. I kept running until my legs began to salt, twitching until I was about to pass out.

​

I looked up and there it was, Abbey's Cafe. "Victory!" I imagined myself at the top of the stairs with a flour sack cape and my hands up like I was saluting Hitler.

​

"Michael Jordan's flu game! Kilgore Trout!"

​

Someone shouted "Crackhead!" from out their car window as they passed by. I was a bloody mess but not badly injured, mostly dehydrated and emotionally exhausted. They were definitely an elite unit, the birds. But it was over.

​

The waitresses at Abbey's wore tight-fitting tops and spandex. Every half hour music comes on and they all freeze and do a coordinated Macarena-type dance as horny fatass truckers salivate over schoolboy fantasies. It looked like chicken dance time inside the restaurant. I was more interested in the eggs.

​

I didn't go in to eat, I knew they wouldn't serve me in the state that I was in. Later I took the long way home to avoid Michael Clark Duncan and his bitch.

​

I sat down on the walkway steps, staring at the patrons. Trying to convince anybody to take my money and get me a burrito but it didn't happen. After a little while, some nice gentleman came outside and gave me a plate of tacos. Probably thought I was homeless. I might be by tonight but I didn't care.

​

After most everyone left I went in to buy a burrito. Manuel was working the front of the house. "Sorry, you can't be in here like that. You came in here wasted yesterday and made a scene and now this. You gotta leave. And your wife came by here this morning asking for you, she bought you a burrito," he said in a compassionate tone. Before I left I drank some water off someone's table, and left a $20 as the tip.

Conclusive Providence

​

When she awoke from surgery she was alone. Ropes of sunlight shone in between the spaces in the aluminum Venetian blinds. Half asleep and giggly — she rotated her neck around in circles; reminiscent of a baby bird coming out of its shell, eyes wide open, calibrating the brightness. High as a kite, she violently ripped off her tethers and made a b-line for the door, all with a silly, happy sort of Christmas morning grin on her face...

​

When she arrived at Abbey's cafe it was almost closing time. She was a mess, bleeding from the mouth and clothes in tatters (I'm shattered). Manuel, who was working the front of the house spoke to her in a compassionate tone.

​

"Sorry, you can't be in here like that. You came in here wasted last week and made a scene and now this. You gotta leave. And your husband came by here this morning asking for you, he bought you a burrito."

​

"What kind of burrito?"

​

"You gotta go now!" he said in a firmer, yet relaxed tone.

​

She sat down on the walkway steps, staring into space trying to convince anyone who walked by to take her money to buy a burrito before the place closed down but it didn't happen. After a little while, some nice gentleman came outside and gave her a plate of tacos.

​

"Ooh, al pastor! These were the best al pastor tacos I've ever had. A Christmas miracle!" She screamed out loud.

​

"So much more exciting than my husband's boring-ass chorizo and eggs. Stupid chorizo and eggs got me into this. Fuck chorizo and eggs al pastor is my perfect bite. I swear off of you chorizo. For the rest of my life.”
